# Bubbles

Live site: https://bubbles.hotelup.work

## About
Bubbles is a hotel and entertainment complex built around water and wellness, positioned as a place to spend a full day rather than just visit. It combines pools, jacuzzis, a sauna, a hot tub (chan), active recreation, and lounge areas in one space, with accommodation also available for guests who want to stay overnight and continue relaxing the next day. The page also promotes it as a venue for events such as birthdays, parties, family gatherings, and corporate outings.

## Amenities
- Two main pools
- Separate children's pool
- Two jacuzzis
- Sauna
- Hot tub (chan)
- Hydromassage pool
- Volleyball
- Gazebos (альтанки)
- Summer lounge bar
- Rooms/accommodation (four published room formats: terrace apartments, lux studio, family suite with bath, family 3-room)
- Free Wi-Fi
- Parking available
- Breakfast (09:00-11:00, booked separately)
- Check-in 14:00, check-out 11:00
